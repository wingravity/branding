## Permitted without asking

- **Nominative reference.** Naming Wingravity in prose to refer to the company
  or its products — reviews, comparisons, press, documentation, invoices.
- **Unmodified wordmark** in an article, deck or "built with" listing, using the
  files in [`brand/logo/`](brand/logo/) and following the rules in
  [`brand/README.md`](brand/README.md#logo).
- **Linking** to wingravity.com or docs.wingravity.com.

## Not permitted without written permission

- Using "Wingravity" in a product name, company name, domain, app-store listing,
  social handle, or in a way that implies partnership, endorsement or affiliation.
- Modifying the mark: recolouring, redrawing, distorting, adding effects,
  putting it in a box, or building a derivative mark from it.
- Merchandise, or any use on a physical good for sale.
- Registering "Wingravity", or a confusingly similar term, as a trademark,
  domain or handle in any jurisdiction.

## How to write the name

- One word, capital W: **Wingravity**. Not WinGravity, Win Gravity, wingravity.
- Lowercase only inside the wordmark artwork itself.
- Never possessive-hyphenated or pluralised ("Wingravity's platform" is fine;
  "Wingravitys" is not).
- Use the name as an adjective before a noun where it reads naturally:
  "the Wingravity brand system", not "a Wingravity".
- In marketing material, mark the first prominent use: Wingravity™. Body copy
  after that needs no symbol. Do not use ® — the mark is not currently
  registered.
